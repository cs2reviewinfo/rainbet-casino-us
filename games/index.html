What a Rainbet slot session uses

Stake size, paylines, and bonus buys belong to the game panel. During a welcome with a wagering requirement, the max bet is 2% of the deposit, and slots with RTP above 97.4% stay blocked. That rule is about the bonus, not about every session.

Can I play them from the US?

No. The catalogue on this page is for reference. Play is not available from the United States or its territories.

Are welcome spins on every slot?

No. Welcome spins stay on Pragmatic Play. Other slots are ordinary lobby titles.

Do you publish RTP here?

No. We do not print an RTP for these titles on this site. The game panel is the source when the account can open it.

Is there a demo?

Only if that title offers demo, and only in a session the Terms allow.
