Responsible gaming

Gambling should stay inside money and time you can lose. If it is past that, stop. In the United States, the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) is free, confidential, and open 24 hours.

Our product rule is 18 or older. The US restriction applies on top of that. This page does not make the product available.

Tools on the account

Where an account exists, the profile can include deposit limits, loss limits, session reminders, cooling-off, and self-exclusion. Use the control that is actually in the profile. If one is missing, ask chat before you assume it is off. Set the limit before you deposit, not after a session has already gone too far.

  • Deposit limits cap what you can add in a period.
  • Loss limits cap net loss in a period.
  • Reminders mark time in a session.
  • Cooling-off and self-exclusion close play for the period you set.

These tools are for eligible accounts. They are not a path into a restricted country.

Signs to stop

  1. Chasing a loss. Raising stakes to get even.
  2. Hiding play. Or using money set aside for bills.
  3. Playing longer than you planned. And feeling unable to close the tab.
  4. Asking someone else to deposit for you.

If that list sounds current, stop and talk to someone. The helpline above does not require a Rainbet account.

Underage play

We do not want anyone under 18 on the product. Verification can ask for a photo ID. A US legal age for gambling is often higher than 18 in a given state. That does not create access. The location is restricted regardless of age.
