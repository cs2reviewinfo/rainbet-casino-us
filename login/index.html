Account access

Rainbet login

Login is the email and password on an existing account, in a location the Terms allow. A correct password does not override a restricted location. If you are in the United States, the sign-in is refused.

There is no separate Rainbet app login. The browser on the phone uses the same credentials.

Signing in

Use the email on the account and the password you set. One account per person. Do not share the password, and do not type it into a page that arrived in an unexpected message. We do not ask for the password in a direct message.

  1. Open the sign-in screen. In the browser you already use.
  2. Enter the email. The one on the account, not a new one.
  3. Enter the password. Then submit.
  4. Stop if the location is refused. A US location is a refusal, not a typo.
  • The same login opens casino, sports, and the cashier.
  • A second account is not a fix for a locked one.
  • Promotions stay on the account. Login does not reset them.

Rainbet password reset

If the password is gone, use the reset on the sign-in screen. It sends a message to the email on the account. Open that message yourself. A reset does not change a restricted location into an allowed one, and it does not recover an account that failed verification.

After a reset, sign in and check the security details. If you did not request the message, contact Rainbet support from a channel you already trust. Email is support@rainbet.com.

  • Use the reset on our sign-in screen only.
  • Ignore a reset link that you did not request.
  • A new password still has to pass the location check.

Rainbet login on mobile

Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android open the same sign-in. The password manager on the device can fill it. That is still our login, not a store app. Rainbet app access is the browser, described on the app page.

If the phone is on a US network, expect the same refusal as on a desktop. Switching from Wi-Fi to cellular, or the other way around, does not change the country rule.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in to Rainbet?

With the email and password on the account, in the browser, from a location the Terms allow.

Can I log in from the United States?

No. Existing accounts cannot sign in from the United States or its territories.

I forgot the password. What now?

Use the reset on the sign-in screen. It goes to the email on the account. It does not lift a location restriction.

Is mobile login different?

No. Safari and Chrome use the same account. There is no separate app password.

Can support sign me in?

Support can help with a real account problem. Support cannot approve a sign-in from a restricted location.
