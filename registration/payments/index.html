Cards, bank transfer and gift cards

Cards and bank transfer appear for accounts where those methods are enabled. Names on the payment should match the account. A gift card, when the cashier lists that method, funds the balance the same way. It can count toward a qualifying welcome deposit for an eligible account. It is not a Rainbet gift card that we print or sell, and it does not bypass a country rule.

  • Match the name on the payment to the account.
  • Read the method minimum in the cashier, not only the $30 welcome floor.
  • Do not send a gift card code to chat or to email.
  • A US location does not gain a method by switching from crypto to a card.

Withdrawals
