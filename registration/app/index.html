Rainbet mobile access

The lobby, Rainbet slots, live tables, Originals, and Rainbet sports use one responsive layout. Sign-in, Promotions, and the cashier are the same screens, narrowed to the phone. You do not keep a separate mobile balance.

  • iPhone and iPad open Rainbet in Safari.
  • Android phones and tablets open it in Chrome.
  • Add the page to the home screen if you want a shortcut. That shortcut is still the browser.
  • Demo mode appears only when the title offers it.

We do not ask you to sideload a package, to trust a third-party store, or to turn off a device security setting. If a page asks for that in our name, it is not us.
