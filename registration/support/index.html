Before contacting support

Check the page that matches the question first. A missing bonus is often an opt-in or a deposit under $30. A missing spin is often the wrong slot. A refused sign-in from the United States is the country rule, not a bug.

  • Have the email on the account.
  • Have the round id if a game looks wrong.
  • Have a screenshot of the cashier or Promotions if money or a bonus is the topic.
  • Do not send your password, a seed phrase, or a gift card code.
  1. Open chat. From the account, when you can sign in.
  2. Or email. support@rainbet.com if chat is not available.
  3. State the account email and the time. Add the screenshot.
  4. Wait for the reply. Do not open a second account while you wait.

Email Rainbet support

Email support@rainbet.com from the address on the account when you can. Say what you expected and what the screen showed. A withdrawal question should include the asset, the network, and the time you confirmed it. A promotion question should include the tier and whether you opted in before the deposit.

We do not ask you to install remote-access software, and we do not ask for the password. If a message does, it is not our support.

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact Rainbet?

Live chat through the day, or email support@rainbet.com.

Can support open a US account?

No. Support cannot override the restriction on the United States and its territories.

What should I include?

The account email, the time, and a screenshot. Add a round id for a game question. Never send a password.

Who do I call about gambling harm in the US?

1-800-GAMBLER. That line is free, confidential, and open 24 hours.
