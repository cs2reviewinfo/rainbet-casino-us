What the form asks for

The form asks for the details that identify one person. Use the legal name, a real email, and a date of birth that matches the documents you could be asked for later. Shared accounts and second accounts are not allowed.

  • One person and one account.
  • Real details, not a borrowed name.
  • A location the Terms allow. The United States and its territories are not on that list.
  • Age of 18 or older under our product rule. The US restriction still applies over that age.
  • Code hella only if a code field is on the form.

The order we expect

  1. Open the formStop if the location is restricted. Do not retry through a tunnel or a proxy.
  2. Enter your detailsName, email, date of birth, and password.
  3. Add hellaOnly when the code field is visible.
  4. Confirm the emailIf the account asks for that step.
  5. Open PromotionsActivate the welcome before a deposit, where the account exists.

Finishing the form is not a deposit and not a bonus. The Rainbet welcome bonus still needs the opt-in and a qualifying deposit, and only in a permitted region.

Rainbet account verification

We can ask for identity documents to confirm who you are and where you live. The Terms describe a government photo ID, a selfie with that ID, and a bank statement or utility bill. A restricted country fails that check. The United States is on the restricted list used in that process.

Until a requested check is finished, deposits and withdrawals can be limited. A failed or refused check can close the account and forfeit funds, as the Terms state. Send clear photos of your own documents. Do not send someone else's.

  • The name on the document should match the account.
  • A proof of address should be recent if we ask for one.
  • A US document confirms a restricted location. It does not unlock the account.
